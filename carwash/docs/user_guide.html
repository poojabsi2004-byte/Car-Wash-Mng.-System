1. Roles at a Glance

Every person who uses the system is assigned to one Car Wash security group by an administrator, under Settings → Users & Companies → Users. What menus and buttons a person sees is driven entirely by that group — there's nothing to configure per-screen.

RoleWhat they mainly do
Front DeskRegister customers/vehicles, book and manage appointments, run the live queue
Washer / DetailerWork their assigned job cards, check off tasks, submit for QC
QC InspectorPass or fail completed job cards
ManagerEverything Front Desk can do, plus configuration, memberships, productivity/revenue reporting
OwnerEverything Manager can do, across every branch at once, plus cross-branch reporting
Portal CustomerBooks a wash online and views their own booking history — no backend access
A person can be more than one role at once (e.g. a Manager who also washes cars) — just tick both groups on their user record.

2. Front Desk

Registering a walk-in customer and vehicle

  1. Go to Car Wash → Customers & Vehicles → Vehicles → New.
  2. Pick or create the customer, enter the license plate, make, model and type.

Creating an appointment

  1. Car Wash → Appointments → New Appointment.
  2. Select the customer, their vehicle, and add one or more services.
  3. Click Confirm once the services are set.

Running the live queue

Car Wash → Queue shows every appointment for today that isn't finished yet, as a kanban board grouped by status — each card's left border and header icon match its status color. Walk it through with the buttons on each card:

ButtonWhen it appearsWhat it does
Vehicle ArrivedConfirmedStamps arrival time (starts the "waiting time" clock shown on the card)
Start ServiceArrivedAssigns the bay; the bay must be free
ReadyIn ServiceOnly available once the job card has passed QC
CompleteReadyFrees the bay, records material usage, and creates the customer's Sales Order

A card turning red with a warning icon means No-Show or Cancelled; a green check means Done.

3. Washer / Detailer

Working your job cards

Car Wash → Job Cards is filtered to open cards. You'll only ever be offered for assignment if you're in the Washer or Manager group — Front Desk staff can't accidentally assign a non-wash-team person to a job.

  1. Open a job card assigned to you (or ask Front Desk/a Manager to assign it).
  2. Click Start — this begins time tracking automatically; you don't need to do anything else for that.
  3. Under the Tasks tab, tick off each checklist item as you complete it.
  4. Once every task is checked, click Submit for QC.
You only see job cards you're assigned to — this is enforced automatically, not something you need to filter yourself.

4. QC Inspector

Passing or failing a job card

  1. Car Wash → Job Cards, filter/group by status to find cards in Quality Check.
  2. Inspect the vehicle, add QC notes if useful.
  3. Click QC Pass to finish it (this also logs the staff member's time spent, and unblocks Front Desk's "Ready" button on the appointment) — or QC Fail (Rework) to send it back to the washer with every checklist item reset to unchecked.

5. Manager

A Manager sees everything Front Desk, Washer and QC see, plus:

Memberships

Car Wash → Memberships — enroll a customer on a plan, renew early, or cancel. Billing itself runs automatically on a schedule; Renew Now triggers an out-of-cycle billing run on demand.

Configuration

Car Wash → Configuration → Services — the service catalogue, including each service's consumables recipe and estimated duration (this duration is what drives online booking slot lengths).

Reporting

Car Wash → Dashboard — an interactive dashboard with weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly/custom date filters, revenue and wash-count trends, top products by material usage, and productivity/revenue drill-downs restricted to Managers and above.

6. Owner

Everything a Manager can do, but across every branch simultaneously (Odoo's company switcher, top-right, controls which branches are "active" at any moment). The Owner's dashboard adds:

  • Branch Breakdown — revenue and wash counts side-by-side per branch.
  • Staff Leaderboard — cross-branch productivity ranking.
  • Membership & Loyalty Totals and Billing Overdue — company-wide, not per-branch.
Switching which companies are active in the company switcher changes what the dashboard and queue show — this is standard Odoo multi-company behavior, not something specific to this app.

7. Portal Customer (Online Booking)

Customers book from the public website — no backend/internal-user access is granted or needed.

Booking a wash

  1. Click Book a Car Wash in the website's top menu (or go to /car-wash/book). Logging in (or creating a portal account) is required — this is by design.
  2. Choose a branch, a service, and a date.
  3. Pick one of the real-time available slots shown — each one lists how many bays are free at that time.
  4. Choose an existing vehicle, or register a new one inline (plate, make, model, type).
  5. Confirm — you'll land on a confirmation page, and the booking immediately appears on the branch's live queue for staff.

Viewing booking history

Once logged into the portal, My Account → Car Wash Bookings (or /my/bookings) lists every booking made under that account with its status; click into one for the full detail including the services on it.

A customer only ever sees their own bookings and vehicles, even if they've booked at more than one branch — never another customer's, and never anyone else's data at any branch.
